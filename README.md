# Sales & Profitability Dashboard, AdventureWorks (Power BI)

A 3-page Power BI dashboard that answers 12 business questions about sales, profit and customers.

## Context
This was a course project using Microsoft's public **AdventureWorks** sample dataset. The aim was to practise building a proper analytical model and turning it into a dashboard a manager could use.

## Data
- Microsoft AdventureWorks sample data (not real company data)
- 12K orders, 11K customers, 9.19M revenue, 42% profit margin

## What I built
- A **star-schema data model**
- A **3-page Power BI dashboard** answering 12 business questions
- **DAX measures** for month-over-month growth, revenue share by product, and revenue per customer

## Key findings
- The **US drives the most revenue**
- **Australia earns the highest profit**
- Revenue alone points to the wrong market: the biggest market by revenue is not the most profitable one
